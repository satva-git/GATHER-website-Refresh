Workflow Overview

Four integrated steps to total intercompany control

Centralised counterparty transaction posting and intercompany reconciliations with automatching; real time visibility
throughout the reporting period and consolidation working papers integration and validation

Central Posting Hub

Quadruple entry posting engine to keep counterparties in balance. Syncs with Xero and QuickBooks in real time.

Real-Time Visibility

Matrix and timeline views for in-month balance monitoring across all counterparty pairs.

Continuous Reconciliation

Autoreconcile matching intercompany transactions, leaving errors, omissions and FX issues for further review and central correction.

Working Papers Validation

Validate intercompany reconciliation data against your consolidation working papers.

1 Step 1: Central Posting Hub

Our Quadruple-Entry Posting Engine

Built on a quadruple-entry posting framework, the central hub automates intercompany recharges, including across currencies, syncing invoices, bills and journals in real time with Xero and QuickBooks to ensure counterparties remain in balance.

  • Quadruple-entry posting framework
  • Bulk approve proposed intercompany transactions
  • Handles 158 currencies with ease
  • Recharge by General Ledger code, vendor, customer, or tracking category
  • Split Recharges and add flexible mark-ups and discounts
  • Full transaction audit trail and documentation

2 Step 2: Real Time Visibility of Counterparty Pairs

Counterparty balance tracking

Keep track of the status of counterparty pairs in real time, not just at month end. Matrix and timeline views give finance teams instant visibility of in-month activity and outstanding balances.

  • Matrix view – keep track of all the counterparty pairs in a Group by period
  • Timeline view: Keep track of individual counterparty balances across a series of time periods
  • Multi-currency support across all views

3 Step 3: Continuous Reconciliation

Continuous Reconciliations with centralised correction posting

Automated intercompany reconciliation that runs continuously, not just at period close. Identify issues arising between counterparty pairs with full visibility of matched and unmatched items. Resolve errors, omissions or FX issues within GATHER and sync to Xero or QuickBooks.

  • Continuous automatching throughout the period
  • Full Matched and Unmatched item visibility
  • Fix errors, omissions and FX issues from GATHER – and sync invoices, bills or correcting journals to Xero and QuickBooks

4 Step 4: Working Papers Validation

Compare with your Consolidation Working Papers

Validate intercompany reconciliation results against consolidation working papers, ensuring total alignment between intercompany counterparty status and group financial reporting before sign-off.

  • Consolidation Working Papers cross-validation
  • Audit-ready reconciliation evidence
  • Validate FX in Reserves adjustments within the Consolidation Working Papers
