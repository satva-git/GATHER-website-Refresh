# Business profile

Date: 2026-11-25. Sources are the saved live pages, chiefly the homepage, About, and Pricing. Inferences are marked.

## What it is

GATHER.nexus is a multi-entity finance platform for finance teams and fractional CFOs who already use Xero or QuickBooks. The company named in the footer is CFO Tech Limited.

Howard Thompson is named on the About page as the founder, a scale-up CFO who built finance functions across seven countries and four continents for a VC-backed business.

## How it makes money

Monthly subscriptions, paid in advance, by module and by the number of groups. Companies inside a group are described as unlimited. A 30-day trial is offered with no credit card. Currencies on the pricing page: GBP, USD, AUD, EUR, NZD, CAD, ZAR. The fetched price grid did not line up cleanly enough to copy figures into this profile. The prices that count are the ones on https://gather.nexus/pricing/ at launch, re-read from the page source before they are republished.

## Products named on the site

- Group Financial Reporting, including drill-down and a group reporting template
- Complex consolidations: acquisitions, disposals, layered ownership
- Intercompany control: recharges, in-month balances, reconciliations, eliminations
- Group Financial Planning: budgets that roll up to the group
- GATHER AI, on its own page
- Integrations with Xero and QuickBooks

## Who it is for

- Finance teams in multi-entity, often multi-currency, scaling businesses
- Fractional CFOs and outsourced finance teams managing more than one client group

## What a visitor is asked to do

Primary action: Start a 30-day free trial (the app signup). Secondary: log in, contact, request enterprise pricing, watch a video, read a knowledge article.

## Proof on the site, and what must not be treated as fact yet

- Press and partner logos appear on the homepage image list (AccountingWEB and others). They need a check against the original files before they are repeated.
- The About page repeats one quote from “David Mitchell, CFO at Directly Energy” four times. Do not treat this as a verified customer until you confirm the person and the company.
- The About page shows three team cards all named Alexa. These look unfinished. Do not publish them as the team.

## Voice

Direct, product-led, aimed at accountants and CFOs. Heavy use of the words multi-entity, consolidation, intercompany, Xero, and QuickBooks. The redesign should stay in that voice.

## Must not lose

Trial and login, the three modules plus complex consolidations and AI, both audience pages, pricing, Xero and QuickBooks pages, legal pages, every knowledge article, every blog post, product updates, and the docs that are in the sitemap.
