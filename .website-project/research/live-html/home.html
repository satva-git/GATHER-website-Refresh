Frequently asked Questions

  • How does GATHER.nexus ensure my data is secure?

    At GATHER.nexus, we take security seriously. Our customers trust us with their financial data, and we honor that trust by implementing best-in-class security practices across our platform. Ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of your data is at the core of everything we do.

    Hosting and Infrastructure

    The GATHER Platform is hosted entirely on the Microsoft Azure Cloud platform. Azure provides a robust, scalable, and secure infrastructure that supports enterprise-level applications across the globe. Microsoft Azure is certified and compliant with numerous international standards and regulations, including ISO 27001, SOC 2, and GDPR. You can read more about Azure’s security capabilities on the Microsoft Azure Trust Center. 

    All data processing, storage, and services are handled within Azure’s environment, and data is only transferred externally via secure and encrypted connections. 

    Data encryption and SSL

    We employ industry-standard encryption protocols to safeguard your data both in transit and at rest: 

    • All communication between your browser and our services is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) technology. 
    • Data stored within GATHER.nexus remains securely encrypted using Azure’s built-in encryption services. 
    • You will always see the padlock icon in your browser when accessing our application, confirming a
      secure SSL connection. 

    API Security Audits

    GATHER.Nexus is an approved integration partner of Xero, and undergoes regular security audits by their team. These audits ensure that our application
    consistently meets the high security standards required for handling financial data via their APIs. 

    User Accounts and Authentication

    • We use the built-in security and identity management services of Microsoft Azure to handle user accounts and authentication. This ensures that our login system complies with the latest security standards, including secure credential storage, encryption, and access control policies.
    • All users authenticate using a secure username and password combination. It is the user’s responsibility to keep these credentials confidential and never share them or store them in insecure locations.
    • You can invite additional users to access specific reports within the GATHER Platform via email. Make sure you trust the recipients, and you may revoke their access at any time through the user management panel. 

    Data Management and Privacy

    Your data is 100% owned by you. We only store financial data pulled from Xero or QuickBooks that is necessary to generate reports and insights within the GATHER
    Platform. 

    • All data is stored within Azure’s secure data zones and is never transferred outside the platform. 
    • When you delete a company from your account, all related financial data and API access tokens are fully and permanently deleted from our systems. 
    • If you cancel your subscription, all stored data will be securely deleted after your billing cycle ends. 

    Access to your data by our support team (employees and/or contractors) is strictly limited and only granted when necessary to provide technical support. Such access is always performed over secure connections and is never shared with any other parties. 

    Payment Security

    GATHER.nexus does not store your credit card or billing details directly. All payment processing is securely handled through our third-party provider, Stripe, which is PCI-DSS compliant and follows the highest standards for handling payment data. 

    For more information about how we protect your data or to request specific details, please contact our support team or visit the GATHER.nexus Help Centre. 

  • How does GATHER AI ensure my data is protected?

    GATHER AI is underpinned by Microsoft Azure AI. Our first AI feature enables auto-mapping from underlying Xero or QuickBooks General Ledger accounts to Group Reporting Templates (for P&L and Balance Sheet) on the GATHER Platform based on GATHER AI’s pre-defined training. No customer-specific financial data is shared with the AI engine. Only the account name, account ID and account type are shared in encrypted format . 

  • What is a Multi-Entity business and how does GATHER.nexus help them?

    The Xero and QuickBooks accounting packages handle the accounting for single entities very well . Businesses on a growth journey – many of which will be VC or PE backed – typically reach a stage at which they are operating across more than one legal entity. 

    Additional legal entities are typically added to support overseas expansion – which often brings the further complexity of operating using multiple currencies. 

    The GATHER Platform delivers applications across three key areas to support Finance teams as they move into the multi-entity environment – or operate in an increasingly complex multi-entity environment: 

    • Group (Consolidated) Financial Reporting 
    • Group Financial Control (including Intercompany Control) 
    • Group (Consolidated) Financial Planning  

  • What type of businesses benefit from GATHER.nexus?

    The GATHER Platform has been built to help Finance teams in businesses with a multi-entity corporate structure. The Platform integrates with the Xero and QuickBooks core accounting systems . Our features will particularly interest Finance teams in venture or PE backed scale-up businesses
    – and Fractional CFOs who work with such businesses .  

  • How does GATHER.nexus integrate with Xero and QuickBooks?

    GATHER.nexus offers seamless integration with Xero and QuickBooks with a single click , allowing you to import and manage your financial data effortlessly across all your legal entities. Users may create Groups of legal entities which are a mixture of Xero and QuickBooks entities if they wish.    

  • How long is the free trial for GATHER.nexus?

    Users may access a 30 day Free Trial Period – during which they have access to all the features of the GATHER Platform – as well as benefiting from regular new features added to the Platform.  

  • What pricing packages do you offer?

    Pricing packages are tailored according to (a) which of our multi-entity applications you wish to use (b) how many Groups of companies you
    wish to manage on our Platform . 

    In each case, the user will pay a monthly subscription fee .

    Further details may be found on our pricing page .

  • Can I upgrade or downgrade my pricing plan later?

    You can upgrade at any time – fees are paid monthly in advance . 

    Equally if you decide to downgrade – or cancel your plan, you may do so with 14 days notice and your current plan will elapse at the end of the month in which the notice period completes .
