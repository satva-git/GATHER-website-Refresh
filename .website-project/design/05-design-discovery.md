# Design discovery

Date: 2026-11-25. No new visual system is started. That waits for your approval of the direction, then a real preview.

## Already decided by the existing brand

- Colours in the refresh: ink `#0a1b30`, paper white, teal `#2d7261`. Keep them. They already distinguish the site from the purple-gradient look of generic templates.
- Type in the refresh: Instrument Sans. One family is enough. Body text should be at least 16px. The current stylesheet uses 15px and should be raised when the design system is built.
- Imagery: product screens from the app, plus the partner marks you have rights to use. No stock people unless you supply them.
- Dark mode: not requested. Leave it out.

## Feel to design toward

Precise, calm, and financial. The first screen should say who it is for and what to do next. The product picture should be a real screen, not a decorative illustration.

## What I will not do until you approve the direction

Build a style-guide page, restyle the homepage, or invent a second visual option.
