GATHER.nexus vs Joiin: which fits your multi-entity finance team?

Compare GATHER.nexus and Joiin for Xero and QuickBooks groups: intercompany control, consolidation, reporting, planning and Excel workflows.

GATHER.nexus and Joiin both support consolidated reporting for Xero and QuickBooks Online groups. Joiin is worth considering when reporting, a broader range of accounting integrations and live Excel access lead your requirements.

The right choice depends on how much of your group finance process you want to manage in one platform, alongside the reports you need to produce.

A major upgrade: set up once across the intercompany workflow

GATHER connects intercompany recharge, reconciliation and consolidation eliminations in one workflow, with linked setup and consolidation working papers. GATHER’s latest Intercompany Control and Group Financial Reporting upgrade brings recharge, reconciliation and consolidation eliminations into one connected workflow. Finance teams and outsourcers managing Xero and QuickBooks Online groups can create linked reconciliations and eliminations as they configure a recharge.

Relevant accounts, contacts and tracking selections carry through automatically, covering both balance sheet and P&L sides. This reduces repeated configuration and helps keep operational intercompany control aligned with group reporting.

The upgrade also includes:

  • Bulk recharge setup: configure multiple ledger/vendor recharges in one journey.
  • More flexible scheduling: use shared or individual schedules, with separate ledger pickup and posting timings.
  • Supporting evidence for recharges: document the basis of a recharge alongside the workflow.
  • Intercompany Overview: see which controls are configured between group companies.

For the Joiin comparison, the key question is how much of this connected process you need alongside consolidated reporting. Ask both providers to demonstrate setup, posting, reconciliation and elimination using the same example; an elimination feature alone does not establish an equivalent end-to-end workflow.

When GATHER.nexus is a strong fit

You want to resolve intercompany differences before reporting

If your team spends month-end finding missing counterparty entries, investigating FX differences and reconciling balances, a consolidated report is only part of the requirement.

GATHER provides central posting into Xero and QuickBooks, transaction automatching and visibility of unmatched items. Finance teams can investigate differences during the reporting period and validate reconciliations against consolidation working papers. Explore Intercompany Control.

Your group structure requires more advanced accounting

Acquisitions and ownership changes introduce calculations that go beyond adding entity balances together. GATHER’s published complex consolidation workflow covers goodwill, fair value adjustments, partial ownership and other acquisition-related accounting. Explore Complex Consolidations.

You need evidence behind the consolidated numbers

GATHER connects consolidation journals, working papers, transaction-level investigation and period sign-off. This gives finance teams a structured place to review how the group result was prepared. Explore Group Financial Reporting.

When Joiin is worth considering

Joiin deserves a place on your shortlist if you need a wider range of accounting connections or want to pull consolidated data into Excel through formulas. Its published integrations include Sage, FreeAgent and other ledgers alongside Xero and QuickBooks Online.

Joiin also supports intercompany eliminations and forecasting. Its account-plus-contact approach can remove selected intercompany amounts while retaining other activity in the same account. Avoid choosing between these platforms on the assumption that Joiin only supports full-account eliminations. See Joiin’s elimination documentation.

Compare the complete cost of your workflow

Compare subscriptions for the same entity count, currencies, modules and user requirements. Then include the time and any separate tools needed for intercompany posting, reconciliations, adjustments and reporting.

Ask both providers to demonstrate a complete month-end using your own transactions. A useful test includes a shared-cost recharge, a counterparty mismatch, an FX difference and the resulting consolidated report.

Frequently asked questions

What does GATHER’s connected intercompany setup do?

Finance teams can create linked reconciliations and eliminations while configuring a recharge. Relevant setup selections carry through automatically, connecting operational intercompany processes with group reporting and reducing repeated configuration.

Is GATHER.nexus an alternative to Joiin?

Yes. GATHER.nexus is an alternative for Xero and QuickBooks Online groups seeking consolidated reporting alongside operational intercompany control and structured consolidation working papers. Joiin should also be evaluated where broader ledger coverage or live Excel reporting is a priority.

Does Joiin support intercompany eliminations?

Yes. Joiin documents account-based and account-plus-contact eliminations. These should be distinguished from creating matching entries in source ledgers and reconciling counterparties before consolidation.

Does Joiin have an Excel add-in?

Yes. Joiin publishes an Excel add-in that retrieves consolidated financial data using Joiin-specific spreadsheet functions. Confirm the required subscription and supported functions for your intended workbook.

Which is better for complex group structures?

GATHER explicitly documents advanced ownership and acquisition accounting. For either platform, test your actual ownership history and required adjustments; partial ownership reporting and acquisition accounting can involve different requirements.
