How to set up multi-GAAP reporting for one company in Xero or QuickBooks Online

If your company keeps its books in Xero or QuickBooks Online and needs to report under a second accounting framework, you can use GATHER.nexus as a multi-GAAP reporting ledger.

If your company keeps its books in Xero or QuickBooks Online and needs to report under a second accounting framework, you can use GATHER.nexus as a multi-GAAP reporting ledger. Set up the company as a single-entity group, select its local ledger currency as the group reporting currency, map its accounts to a Group Reporting Template (GRT), and post manual GAAP adjustments in that same currency.

For example, a UK subsidiary can maintain its books under UK GAAP in Xero and use GATHER.nexus to prepare an IFRS reporting pack in GBP for its parent company. The local books stay in Xero, and GATHER.nexus records the adjustments that bridge the local trial balance to the IFRS figures.

What is a multi-GAAP reporting ledger

A multi-GAAP reporting ledger lets a finance team report the same company under more than one accounting framework. In this setup, Xero or QuickBooks Online holds the local accounting records, and GATHER.nexus provides a second reporting view by applying documented adjustments to the mapped local balances.

GAAP-adjusted reporting balances = mapped local balances + manual GAAP adjustments

1: Set up the company as a single-entity group

Create a group in GATHER.nexus and add the company from Xero or QuickBooks Online as its only entity. Set the group reporting currency to the currency in which the company keeps its books. If the company’s ledger is in GBP, set the group reporting currency to GBP.

Keeping both views in the same currency lets you reconcile the mapped local balances and the adjusted balances directly. If the parent also requires figures in a different currency, treat currency translation as a separate step.

2: Map the local chart of accounts to the Group Reporting Template

Use a Group Reporting Template (GRT) that reflects the financial statements or reporting pack you need to produce. If your parent company specifies reporting lines, use those lines as the basis for the template. Map each Xero or QuickBooks Online account to its corresponding line, then reconcile the mapped balances back to the source trial balance.

Mapping determines where a balance appears. A GAAP adjustment changes the amount reported on a line. If part of a source account needs to appear on a different reporting line, use a manual reclassification to move the relevant amount.

3: Post manual GAAP adjustments in the local currency

Identify the differences between the local accounting treatment and the target reporting framework. Post each adjustment as a balanced manual journal in GATHER.nexus using the company’s ledger currency.

Adjustments might relate to lease accounting, revenue recognition, or the classification of assets and liabilities. For each journal, record the accounting rationale, period, supporting calculation and any reversal or roll-forward required.

4: Reconcile and review the GAAP-adjusted figures

Review the adjusted balances against the mapped local balances and the journal schedule. In this illustrative example, £30,000 moves from non-current liabilities to current liabilities for the reporting pack.

Reporting lineMapped local balanceManual adjustmentAdjusted balance
Non-current liabilities£150,000−£30,000£120,000
Current liabilities£50,000+£30,000£80,000
Total liabilities shown£200,000£0£200,000

The local ledger remains intact, and the reviewer can trace the £30,000 movement to the GBP journal in GATHER.nexus.

Make multi-GAAP reporting part of the close

At each close, review any new local accounts, refresh the GAAP calculations and check which prior-period adjustments should carry forward, reverse or be recalculated. Before sign-off, reconcile the reporting pack to the mapped local trial balance and the manual adjustment schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use GATHER.nexus for multi-GAAP reporting with one company?

Yes. Add the company as the only entity in a GATHER.nexus group, use its local ledger currency as the reporting currency, map its accounts to a GRT and post manual adjustments for the target reporting framework.

Can I prepare an IFRS reporting pack from a UK GAAP trial balance?

Yes. Use the UK GAAP trial balance from Xero or QuickBooks Online as the starting point. Map it to the parent’s reporting structure and post the adjustments needed for IFRS. The finance team determines and documents the accounting treatment for each adjustment.

Do GAAP adjustments change my Xero or QuickBooks Online balances?

Manual adjustments posted in GATHER.nexus for this workflow affect the GATHER.nexus reporting view. Your Xero or QuickBooks Online ledger continues to hold the local books.
