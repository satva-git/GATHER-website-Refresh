How to set up intercompany recharges in bulk with GATHER.nexus

GATHER.nexus lets finance teams configure multiple intercompany recharges in one setup workflow.

On this page

GATHER.nexus lets finance teams configure multiple intercompany recharges in one setup workflow. Recharge sources include ledger accounts, vendors, Xero Tracking Categories and QuickBooks Online Classes. For groups using Xero or QuickBooks Online, that saves time when a parent company or shared services entity regularly allocates several costs across subsidiaries. See GATHER.nexus Intercompany Control.

What is bulk intercompany recharge setup?

Bulk intercompany recharge setup is the process of configuring several recharge rules together. Each rule defines the costs to recharge, the companies that should bear them and the basis for splitting the amount. In GATHER.nexus, a finance team can configure multiple recharges together using ledger accounts, vendors, Xero Tracking Categories or QuickBooks Online Classes as the source. See GATHER.nexus Intercompany Control.

The distinction matters when several costs follow different allocation policies. Software subscriptions might be split by licence count, HR costs by headcount and insurance by insured exposure. Finance can set up the recharges together while retaining the allocation basis for each cost.

How does bulk recharge setup save time?

WHERE THE WORK HAPPENSWHAT BULK SETUP CHANGES
Initial rolloutConfigure several eligible recharges in one workflow, reducing the time spent opening and completing individual setups.
Moving off spreadsheetsTurn existing allocation policies into recharge rules that can be used again.
Each reporting periodApply configured splits, mark-ups or discounts where appropriate and prepare corresponding entries centrally.

The immediate saving is in setup. The ongoing saving comes from reusing the rules and preparing the postings through a central workflow. Both matter when the same finance team is responsible for several Xero organisations or QuickBooks Online companies.

Example: one shared services company, three cost pools

A shared services company pays for Microsoft 365, external IT support and group insurance on behalf of three subsidiaries. Finance can use bulk setup to configure the vendor-based software and IT recharges alongside a ledger-based insurance recharge. If the group codes shared IT costs to a Xero Tracking Category option or a QuickBooks Online Class, finance can use that dimension to identify the costs to recharge.

Suppose the group allocates £6,000 of software costs using a 50%, 30% and 20% split based on licence counts. The subsidiaries receive £3,000, £1,800 and £1,200 respectively. The IT and insurance recharges can each use their own allocation basis. If licence counts change materially, finance can review the rule before applying it to future periods.

The same setup works whether a group is configuring its first set of recurring recharges or bringing an existing schedule of spreadsheet allocations into GATHER.nexus.

What should you have ready before setting up recharges in bulk?

For each recharge, identify the source company, the source basis (ledger account, vendor, Xero Tracking Category option or QuickBooks Online Class), the receiving entities and the allocation basis. Confirm whether a mark-up or discount applies and whether the resulting entries should be recorded as journals or as intercompany invoices and bills. Having those decisions ready lets the team work through the rules together and gives the reviewer a clear basis for checking them.

Frequently asked questions

Can I set up intercompany recharges in bulk for Xero and QuickBooks Online?

Yes. GATHER.nexus supports bulk setup for recharges based on ledger accounts, vendors, Xero Tracking Categories or QuickBooks Online Classes, and central intercompany posting to connected Xero and QuickBooks Online companies.

What is the difference between bulk recharge setup and bulk approval?

Bulk setup configures multiple recharge rules. Bulk approval lets a reviewer approve several proposed intercompany transactions together. They save time at different stages of the workflow.

Can bulk-configured recharge rules be used for recurring recharges?

GATHER.nexus supports recurring intercompany recharges. Use scheduling for costs that recur and review the proposed amounts and supporting evidence as part of the periodic close.
