How to schedule intercompany recharges in GATHER.nexus

GATHER.nexus helps finance teams schedule recurring intercompany recharges across Xero and QuickBooks Online companies.

On this page

How scheduling works Scheduling dates Documentation Approvals Posting statuses Shorter month-end FAQs

GATHER.nexus helps finance teams schedule recurring intercompany recharges across Xero and QuickBooks Online companies. Teams can record the explanation and supporting documentation for a recharge, review proposed transactions centrally and approve them in bulk before posting the corresponding entries to the connected ledgers. See GATHER.nexus Intercompany Control.

How does a scheduled intercompany recharge work?

For a recurring recharge based on ledger data, GATHER.nexus lets finance choose the source period, the date to pick up that period’s ledger data and the date to use for the resulting postings. The recharge rule defines which costs are picked up, which entities receive the charge and how it is split. You can source costs by ledger account, vendor, Xero Tracking Category option or QuickBooks Online Class. Together, they let finance repeat the workflow for regular management fees, software subscriptions and other shared costs.

StepWhat finance does
1. Configure the ruleSelect the charging company and the cost source, such as a Xero Tracking Category option or QuickBooks Online Class. Choose the receiving companies and the allocation basis. Where relevant, configure a mark-up or discount.
2. Set the scheduleSet the recurrence, choose the source period and specify the ledger pickup date and the posting date.
3. Document the rechargeRecord the purpose of the charge and its calculation basis. Keep period-specific support, such as the supplier invoice or the allocation calculation, with the recharge documentation.
4. Review and approveReview the proposed transactions centrally. Check the source amount, participating companies, allocations and period. Approve multiple proposals together where appropriate.
5. Post to the ledgersPost the corresponding journals or intercompany invoice and bill entries centrally to the connected accounting systems.

What dates can you choose when scheduling a recharge?

Scheduling choiceWhat it controlsExample
Source periodWhich period’s ledger transactions are used to calculate the recharge.Use October software costs coded to the Shared IT Tracking Category option in Xero or Shared IT Class in QuickBooks Online.
Ledger pickup dateWhen GATHER.nexus retrieves the ledger data for that source period.Pick up October costs on 3 November, after finance has entered the October supplier bills.
Posting dateThe transaction date applied to the resulting recharge entries in the connected ledgers.Date the invoice and matching bill 31 October to recognise the recharge in October, while that period is open.

In this example, the team reviews and releases the recharge in November using October source costs and an October transaction date. The pickup date gives finance time to enter late supplier bills. The posting date keeps the recharge in the intended accounting period.

What documentation should accompany a recurring recharge?

A reviewer should be able to see why the charge was made, how it was calculated and which reporting period it covers. For a shared software subscription, that typically means the supplier cost for the period, the list of participating companies and the user counts behind the allocation. If the allocation changes, document what changed and when it takes effect.

GATHER.nexus supports recording recharge documentation and explanations alongside the workflow. Keeping the rationale with the transaction helps the reviewer check the proposal and gives the finance team a reference when a counterparty queries its charge later. See GATHER.nexus on central recharge review and documentation.

How do approvals work for scheduled intercompany recharges?

Finance can review proposed transactions from a central view and bulk approve those that are ready. A practical review checks whether the source cost is complete, whether the split reflects the current allocation basis and whether the entries belong in the intended period. For charges with a mark-up, the reviewer can also check the rate against the group’s policy.

For example, if a supplier adds 20 licences mid-month, the reviewer can check the updated licence count and the period covered by the invoice before approving the proposal. That gives the team a way to handle changes while keeping the recurring process on the close calendar.

What posting statuses are available in Xero and QuickBooks Online?

Central approval in GATHER.nexus lets Group finance review the allocation and release the proposed entries. Where the destination ledger offers a suitable status or approval workflow, the local finance team can carry out a second review.

Destination and transactionLedger-level statuses and review
Xero invoices and billsXero supports Draft, Awaiting Approval and Approved (shown under Awaiting Payment for unpaid items). Draft leaves the entry available for editing and review. Awaiting Approval puts it in the local approval queue. Approved entries are reflected in the accounts. Xero invoice statuses · Xero bill statuses
Xero manual journalsXero supports Draft and Posted. A draft journal can be reviewed in Xero before it is posted. Xero manual journals
QuickBooks Online invoices and billsEligible QuickBooks Online Advanced companies can configure native approval workflows. Invoices can move to Pending Approval and then Approved. Bills that meet the workflow conditions can be queued as Pending Approval for the local approver. Invoice approval can govern release to the customer. Bill approval and payment release can be separate steps. QuickBooks invoice approvals · QuickBooks bill approvals

Before relying on the second approval step, confirm which posting statuses GATHER.nexus exposes for the chosen transaction type and, in QuickBooks Online, whether the company’s configured approval workflow picks up entries created by the integration.

How does scheduling shorten month-end?

Scheduling reduces the work of recreating recurring charges and remembering when they are due. Finance can focus on changes in the source data and on reviewing the proposals. Central posting then records the corresponding entries across the entities, giving the team a starting point for intercompany reconciliation before group reporting.

Frequently asked questions

Can I schedule a monthly intercompany recharge for Xero or QuickBooks Online?

Yes. GATHER.nexus supports recurring recharges and central posting to connected Xero and QuickBooks Online companies. Monthly management fees and shared subscription costs are common use cases.

Can the amount vary from period to period?

Yes. A recurring recharge can use costs sourced from the connected ledger, including transactions coded to a Xero Tracking Category option or QuickBooks Online Class. The amount can vary with the costs picked up for each period. The configured split or mark-up can be applied to the amount for the period.

Can I pick up last month’s costs after the period ends and date the recharge to that month?

Yes. Choose the prior month as the source period, schedule the pickup after the supplier costs have been recorded, and set the recharge posting date within that month, subject to the destination ledger’s period locks.

Can I approve multiple proposed intercompany recharges together?

Yes. GATHER.nexus supports bulk approval of proposed intercompany transactions. Review the amounts and supporting evidence before approving the proposals that are ready to post.
