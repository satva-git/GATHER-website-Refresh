The key difference is scope: Mayday focuses on intercompany and month-end automation, while GATHER connects intercompany recharges and reconciliation directly to consolidation, Group reporting and planning.

If your requirement ends with creating and reconciling intercompany transactions, both platforms offer specialist automation.

What is the main difference between GATHER and Mayday?

The main difference between GATHER.nexus and Mayday is where the intercompany workflow ends.

Mayday provides specialist tools for intercompany recharges, balancing and other month-end accounting processes.

GATHER treats intercompany accounting as the first part of the wider Group accounting process.

This allows the finance team to follow an intercompany position from the originating accounting transaction through to its treatment in the consolidated Group accounts.

Intercompany recharge automation

GATHER Intercompany Recharge

GATHER uses a central quadruple-entry posting framework to automate corresponding accounting entries between Group entities.

Finance teams can create recharge rules using:

  • General Ledger accounts
  • Xero tracking categories
  • QuickBooks Online classes
  • Customers
  • Vendors
  • Existing customer or vendor transactions

Recharge amounts can be split between multiple entities and include configurable mark-ups or discounts.

GATHER can then create the corresponding:

  • Sales invoices
  • Purchase bills
  • Journals

across the relevant connected entities.

The objective is to create both sides of the intercompany relationship from one controlled Group-level workflow rather than requiring finance teams to manage reciprocal entries separately.

Mayday Recharger

Mayday Recharger also automates the allocation and posting of costs and revenues between Group entities.

Users can create recharge rules and apportion amounts between entities, with postings made back to the underlying accounting systems.

For Xero organisations, Mayday supports invoice-and-bill or journal posting.

For QuickBooks Online, Mayday currently documents Recharger posting as journal-only.

GATHER or Mayday for intercompany recharges?

Both platforms provide substantial recharge automation.

GATHER may be particularly relevant where a Group requires consistent Xero and QuickBooks functionality, customer/vendor-driven recharge workflows, or wants the resulting intercompany accounting to feed directly into its consolidation process.

Intercompany reconciliation

Creating intercompany entries is only half of the problem.

Multi-entity finance teams also need to determine whether the reciprocal positions recorded by the two entities actually agree.

Continuous intercompany reconciliation in GATHER

GATHER continuously matches intercompany transactions throughout the reporting period.

Finance teams can see:

  • Matched transactions
  • Unmatched transactions
  • Outstanding counterparty differences
  • FX-related discrepancies
  • Errors and omissions requiring correction

Corrections can be created centrally in GATHER and posted back into Xero or QuickBooks Online.

This means intercompany reconciliation does not have to begin as a month-end spreadsheet exercise.

Group-wide counterparty visibility

GATHER provides two views designed specifically for Group intercompany control.

Counterparty Matrix

The Matrix provides a Group-wide view of counterparty pairs for a selected reporting period.

Finance teams can identify which intercompany relationships agree and where differences remain outstanding.

Counterparty Timeline

The Timeline follows an individual counterparty relationship across multiple accounting periods.

This gives controllers visibility not only of the current balance but also of how that relationship has developed over time.

Together, these tools move intercompany accounting from a period-end checking exercise toward continuous Group-level control.

From intercompany reconciliation to consolidation

This is one of the most important differences between GATHER and Mayday.

Reconciled balances eventually have to be eliminated from the consolidated Group accounts.

In GATHER, intercompany reconciliation and Group consolidation sit within the same platform.

Finance teams can validate reconciliation results against the Consolidation Working Papers, review the relevant elimination calculations and investigate differences before Group reporting is signed off.

The result is not simply an intercompany reconciliation. It is an audit-ready record of how the intercompany position has been treated in the Group accounts.

GATHER vs Mayday for QuickBooks Online

Both GATHER and Mayday integrate with QuickBooks Online, but the current scope of their intercompany functionality differs.

GATHER supports invoices, bills and journals as part of its QuickBooks Online intercompany recharge workflow.

Mayday currently states that its QuickBooks Online Recharger implementation posts recharges as journals rather than invoices and bills.

Mayday also states that QuickBooks Online classes and tags are not currently supported across its products.

For Groups that operate substantially on QuickBooks Online, this difference may be important when evaluating intercompany software.

GATHER vs Mayday for Xero

Both GATHER and Mayday provide substantial intercompany automation for Xero Groups.

Mayday has particularly strong functionality around Xero-based intercompany workflows and Group master-data management through Mayday HQ.

GATHER’s main distinction is what happens after the intercompany accounts have been reconciled.

The reconciled position can flow into GATHER’s:

  • Automated eliminations
  • Consolidation Working Papers
  • Complex consolidation calculations
  • Group reporting
  • Group planning

For a Xero Group looking only to automate intercompany processes, both platforms warrant consideration.

For a Group looking for a wider Group accounting platform, the products solve different scopes of problem.

More than intercompany accounting

GATHER is designed as a multi-entity finance platform rather than a standalone intercompany application.

Complex Group Consolidation

GATHER handles Group accounting scenarios including:

  • Acquisitions
  • Disposals
  • Goodwill
  • Fair value adjustments
  • Partial acquisitions
  • Non-controlling interests
  • Step acquisitions
  • Multi-level and chain ownership structures
  • Manual Group-level adjustments

Consolidation Working Papers

GATHER maintains digital Consolidation Working Papers containing the calculations supporting the consolidated accounts.

Finance teams can move from a reported number through the relevant consolidation calculation and down to the underlying accounting information.

This provides a permanent digital audit trail rather than relying on offline consolidation spreadsheets.

Group Financial Reporting

GATHER produces Group reporting across multiple entities, currencies, divisions and functions.

Reports support multi-level drill-down, allowing finance teams to move from the Group result into entity-level and underlying transaction detail.

Group AP/AR Ageing

GATHER provides Group-wide Accounts Receivable and Accounts Payable ageing analysis.

Finance teams can review working-capital exposure across the Group and drill from the Group summary through individual entities to transaction-level detail.

Group Financial Planning

Budgets can be created across entities, divisions, regions and functions and rolled up into a consolidated Group plan.

Actual-versus-budget reporting then uses the same Group structures as the underlying financial reporting.

Which platform is designed for the wider Group finance workflow?

Mayday has expanded beyond intercompany accounting into broader month-end activities including accruals, prepayments, deferred revenue, flux analysis and close management.

GATHER has expanded in a different direction.

The choice therefore depends partly on the problem the finance team wants to solve.

Mayday is designed around automating month-end accounting workflows.

GATHER is designed around operating and controlling finance across a multi-entity Group.

Built for Fractional CFOs and outsourced finance teams

GATHER can also support finance professionals responsible for multiple separate client Groups.

Fractional CFOs, accounting firms and outsourced finance teams can manage multiple Groups from a single GATHER interface.

Access can be controlled by Group, entity and feature.

This allows an outsourced finance team to standardise its Group accounting processes across its client portfolio without combining client data or creating separate technology stacks for every engagement.

What happens when a Fractional CFO hands the client in-house?

GATHER is designed to support the transition from outsourced to internal finance.

A Fractional CFO or outsourced finance provider can establish the client’s:

  • Group structure
  • Chart-of-accounts mappings
  • Intercompany relationships
  • Recharge rules
  • Consolidation methodology
  • Working Papers
  • Reporting structures
  • Planning processes

If the client subsequently appoints its own CFO or builds an internal finance team, control of that Group can be handed over.

The incoming finance team can continue using the existing GATHER environment.

No reporting model rebuild.
No new consolidation system.
No loss of historical working papers.
No break in the Group accounting process.
