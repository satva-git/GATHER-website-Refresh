Connect recharge setup to reconciliation and elimination

GATHER’s major Intercompany Control and Group Financial Reporting upgrade extends the comparison beyond report output.

As finance teams configure a recharge, they can create linked reconciliations and consolidation eliminations, carrying relevant accounts, contacts and tracking selections through the workflow for both balance sheet and P&L items.

Bulk recharge setup and shared or individual schedules help teams configure recurring activity, including separate ledger pickup and posting timings.

Supporting evidence documents the basis of recharges, while the Intercompany Overview shows which controls are configured between companies.

Consider a recurring management recharge: your evaluation should cover how it is configured, posted, reconciled and eliminated, as well as how its impact appears in a management report.

GATHER’s connected setup is particularly relevant when the repeated work before reporting is a significant part of your team’s workload.

This is a workflow distinction to test in a demonstration. Fathom’s documented elimination and forecasting features should be assessed on their own merits, alongside your requirements for operational intercompany control.

When GATHER.nexus is a strong fit

Intercompany control is a recurring month-end bottleneck

GATHER brings counterparty posting and reconciliation into the same platform as group reporting. Its matrix and timeline views help teams monitor balances throughout the month, while reconciliation results can be checked against consolidation working papers.

For teams managing frequent recharges or unexplained differences, this is a practical reason to evaluate GATHER. Explore the intercompany workflow.

You need a structured consolidation close

GATHER’s reporting workflow includes group account mapping, automated journals, working paper review and sign-off.

It provides a place to investigate adjustments before producing the final group reports. Explore the reporting workflow.

Acquisitions or layered ownership make consolidation harder

GATHER publishes support for acquisitions, disposals, NCI, step acquisitions and chain holdings. Ask to see your ownership structure demonstrated, including the calculations and resulting journals. Explore advanced consolidation accounting.

When Fathom is worth considering

Fathom is a compelling option to evaluate when your main deliverables are management reports, performance insights and cash flow forecasts. Its published features include configurable report content and three-way forecasting.

For consolidated forecasts, Fathom documents two approaches: forecasting directly at group level, or bringing underlying company forecast P&L values into the group through its budget-link workflow.

Entity forecasts do not automatically roll up in full; group balance sheet forecasting uses the group’s own settings. See Fathom’s group forecasting guide.

GATHER’s published planning proposition centres on collaborative budgets and variance analysis. Teams whose primary requirement is three-way cash flow forecasting should test Fathom’s forecasting workflow closely.

How do the elimination workflows differ?

Fathom can automatically eliminate entire accounts dedicated to intercompany activity. For selected amounts within mixed accounts, its documentation describes a separate Eliminations Company with manual entries each period.

GATHER generates recurring consolidation journals and provides working papers for review.

When evaluating either approach, test a mixed trade/intercompany account, an unmatched balance and a currency difference. Check how the result is explained and carried into the next period.
