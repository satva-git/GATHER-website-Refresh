Guide

Can fractional CFOs give clients report access without exposing other engagements?

Yes. A fractional CFO platform can give clients access to their own financial reports if it lets the firm control which client groups each user can access and what they can do within those groups.

How does GATHER.nexus keep client reporting separate?

Set up each unrelated client engagement as a separate Group. In GATHER.nexus, a Group brings together the entities that belong in a client’s consolidated reporting, whether they use Xero, QuickBooks Online or a mix of both.

When you invite a client user, select the specific Group they should access. The fractional CFO and their team can have access to multiple Groups as their assignments require.

ControlWhat it governsExample
Group accessWhich client Groups a user can accessA client director gets access to their own Group only.
Role permissionsWhich features and actions a user can useA board member gets Report Viewer access. A controller gets permissions suited to their finance responsibilities.
Group Class accessWhich reporting segments within an authorised Group a user can accessA regional manager is assigned the UK Group Class for their own Group.

Group access answers which client’s data can this person access? The assigned role answers what can this person see or do? GATHER.nexus documents a Report Viewer role that provides read-only access to generated reports and excludes transaction-level visibility.

A client controller who helps prepare the close can have a different role from a board member who just reviews the consolidated results.

Group Classes are useful when reporting responsibilities are narrower than the whole client Group.

For example, a regional lead may need to review their region’s results across several subsidiaries. Groups provide the boundary between separate client engagements. Group Classes apply within that boundary.

See Inviting & Managing Team Members, Set up Roles & Permissions and Assigning Group Class Values.

What does this look like in practice?

Imagine a fractional CFO firm working with two unrelated clients. One is a software business with a UK parent and a US subsidiary.

The other is a retail group with three trading entities. The firm manages both engagements in GATHER.nexus.

It invites the software client’s CEO with Report Viewer access and assigns only the software Group.

The CEO can review that Group’s consolidated reports. The retail Group sits outside the CEO’s assigned access.

If the software business hires a controller who will help prepare the group close, the firm can invite them to the same Group with permissions suited to that job.

The CEO retains their reporting role. Before rolling access out more widely, the firm can check what a user with each intended role can see, including whether the appropriate reports and level of detail are available.

What happens when the client hires a full-time CFO?

GATHER.nexus lets the account’s primary admin transfer ownership of an existing Group to another user.

  1. In Multi-Entity & Display → Group, open the client’s Group and choose Transfer Ownership.
  2. Enter the recipient’s email. Choose whether to preserve current users’ access and permissions or reset permissions under the new owner.
  3. Review the ownership details and the modules shown as remaining enabled, then send the transfer.
  4. Check the transfer status. The recipient gains control of the Group once they accept.

This gives a fractional CFO firm a way to hand over control of the client’s reporting Group when the client hires a permanent CFO or takes reporting in-house.

At handover, agree who still needs access. If the fractional CFO is staying on as an adviser, their ongoing access can reflect that narrower role.

If the engagement is ending, the permissions review is an opportunity to remove access that is no longer needed. Transfer Ownership help guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can clients view their consolidated reports without editing them?

Yes. Assign the Report Viewer role and limit Group access to the client’s own Group. The role provides read-only access to generated reports.

Can a fractional CFO use one platform for multiple Xero and QuickBooks Online clients?

Yes. In GATHER.nexus, a firm can manage multiple Groups from one interface and assign users to the specific Groups they need. Each client Group can contain the relevant entities for its own consolidated reporting.

Can a fractional CFO hand over a single client Group?

Yes. The primary admin initiates the transfer for the individual Group. Ownership changes when the recipient accepts, and the sender can choose whether to preserve or reset existing user permissions as part of that transfer.
