Multi-ledger, multi-entity consolidation: bringing Xero and QuickBooks Online groups together

A group does not need to use the same accounting platform across every company to produce consolidated accounts. GATHER.nexus connects to both Xero and QuickBooks Online, bringing entities from either platform into one group reporting and consolidation workflow.

For CFOs and financial controllers, this means the group can retain the accounting systems that work for individual businesses while applying consistent financial reporting across the whole organisation.

Whether your group uses Xero throughout, QuickBooks Online throughout, or a mixture of both, GATHER.nexus provides a unified multi-entity layer for intercompany control, group financial reporting and planning.

What is multi-ledger, multi-entity consolidation?

Multi-entity consolidation combines the financial information of separate legal entities into a single set of group financial statements.

Multi-ledger consolidation extends that process to groups whose entities use different accounting systems. For example, a parent company and two subsidiaries might use Xero, while another subsidiary uses QuickBooks Online.

The accounting principles remain the same. The group must bring together each entity’s financial information, apply consistent reporting classifications, translate currencies where necessary and process consolidation adjustments and eliminations.

The practical challenge is making information from different systems work together reliably.

Multi-ledger describes the accounting systems in use. Multi-entity describes the companies being consolidated. Groups may need to manage both dimensions at the same time.

Why groups end up using both Xero and QuickBooks Online

Mixed accounting systems are often a natural consequence of growth.

An acquired business may already use QuickBooks Online while the rest of the group runs on Xero. An overseas subsidiary may have chosen its platform to suit its local accountant. Individual businesses may have made their accounting decisions before a central finance function existed.

Standardising every company onto one ledger can be a substantial project. It may involve migrating historical data, replacing integrations, retraining staff and changing established processes.

For some groups, that investment makes sense. For others, the immediate priority is to establish dependable group reporting.

Using both Xero and QuickBooks Online does not, by itself, require a migration before consolidation can begin. GATHER.nexus connects to both platforms so the finance team can build a common reporting framework above the underlying ledgers.

Why exporting both systems into Excel becomes difficult

A spreadsheet can combine financial information from Xero and QuickBooks Online. The difficulty is maintaining a controlled, repeatable consolidation process as the group grows.

Finance teams typically need to:

  • Export information from each company.
  • Map different account codes into a common reporting structure.
  • Check that reporting periods and classifications align.
  • Translate foreign currency results and balances.
  • Identify and reconcile intercompany differences.
  • Record consolidation adjustments and eliminations.
  • Explain how the final group numbers were calculated.

Each step introduces work that must be repeated, reviewed and documented. When a subsidiary posts a late adjustment, the team must establish which exports, formulas and working papers need updating.

The issue is therefore broader than combining two file formats. It is maintaining consistency and traceability from the underlying company accounts to the final consolidated result.

How GATHER.nexus brings Xero and QuickBooks Online together

GATHER.nexus connects to both Xero and QuickBooks Online and provides a common environment for managing the group’s financial information.

An entity’s choice of accounting platform does not need to determine how it appears in group reporting. Finance teams can organise information around the group’s reporting requirements while retaining the underlying connection to each company’s ledger.

1. Map different charts of accounts into one group reporting structure

Companies rarely use identical charts of accounts—even when they use the same accounting software.

One business may split revenue by service line. Another may use a smaller set of sales accounts. Expense codes and account names can differ across subsidiaries.

GATHER.nexus’s Group Reporting Templates provide a common reporting structure into which entity accounts can be mapped. Bulk mapping and AI-assisted mapping help finance teams establish consistent classifications across the group.

This allows the group to report consistently without requiring every subsidiary to adopt identical account codes first.

2. Consolidate across currencies

A mixed Xero and QuickBooks Online group may also operate across several currencies.

GATHER.nexus supports multi-currency group reporting, allowing finance teams to bring entities with different functional currencies into a common reporting currency. Where required, teams can use their own exchange rates.

This brings currency translation into the consolidation process alongside account mapping and group adjustments.

3. Manage intercompany activity across the group

A common group reporting structure does not automatically resolve intercompany differences.

One company may record a balance in a different period, use an inconsistent exchange rate or omit the corresponding transaction. These issues can arise within a single accounting platform as well as between different platforms.

GATHER.nexus brings intercompany recharge, reconciliation and consolidation eliminations into a connected workflow. Finance teams can create linked reconciliations and eliminations as they configure recharges, supported by a shared configuration.

That connection helps the team manage how intercompany activity is posted, checked and removed from consolidated results.

4. Record consolidation adjustments with supporting working papers

Consolidation involves more than adding together subsidiary trial balances.

The group may need to record accounting policy adjustments, acquisition entries, goodwill, non-controlling interests or other consolidation treatments.

GATHER.nexus’s Consolidation Working Papers provide a structured environment for these calculations and adjustments, with calculation cards, drill-down and a permanent digital audit trail.

This gives finance teams a place to explain the movement from the underlying company accounts to the consolidated group position.

5. Investigate the numbers behind group reports

A consolidated figure is more useful when the finance team can explain its composition.

GATHER.nexus’s reporting and working-paper drill-down help users investigate contributing entities and underlying financial information. That makes it easier to answer questions such as:

  • Which subsidiary drove the movement in group operating expenses?
  • Has an intercompany balance been fully reconciled?
  • What adjustment explains the difference between entity totals and the consolidated result?
  • How much of a reported movement relates to currency translation?

The purpose is to make group reporting easier to review, explain and use.

Example: consolidating an acquisition that uses QuickBooks Online

Consider a group with three existing companies on Xero that acquires a business using QuickBooks Online.

The finance team needs to include the acquisition in group reporting while the acquired business continues operating its established accounting processes.

With GATHER.nexus, the team can connect the QuickBooks Online entity alongside the existing Xero entities, map its chart of accounts into the group reporting structure and incorporate it into the consolidation workflow.

Where relevant, the team can also apply currency translation, reconcile intercompany balances and record acquisition-related consolidation adjustments.

The group can establish consolidated reporting while assessing its longer-term accounting system strategy separately.

This is an illustrative scenario; the accounting treatment depends on the acquisition date, ownership structure and applicable reporting framework.

One reporting framework, with local accounting flexibility

There is a useful distinction between standardising accounting systems and standardising financial reporting.

Standardising accounting systems means moving entities onto the same underlying software.

Standardising financial reporting means applying consistent classifications, consolidation treatments and reporting processes across the group.

GATHER.nexus allows Xero and QuickBooks Online groups to pursue the second objective while retaining flexibility over the first.

Individual companies can continue using their existing platform, while the central finance team establishes a shared framework for group reporting and control.

Connecting consolidation with planning

The value of a common group structure extends beyond historical reporting.

Once actual results are organised consistently, finance teams have a clearer basis for comparing performance against budgets and plans.

GATHER.nexus brings group financial reporting and planning into the same platform, supporting actual-versus-budget analysis across the group and by dimensions such as division, region or function.

For groups using both Xero and QuickBooks Online, this helps connect information from the underlying accounting systems to a broader view of group performance.

Frequently asked questions

Can you consolidate Xero and QuickBooks Online companies together?

Yes. GATHER.nexus connects to both Xero and QuickBooks Online, allowing companies using either platform to be included in the same group reporting and consolidation workflow.

Does every entity need to use the same accounting software?

No. GATHER.nexus supports groups using Xero, QuickBooks Online or a mixture of both. A common reporting structure can be applied above the underlying accounting systems.

Do all companies need an identical chart of accounts?

No. Different entity charts of accounts can be mapped into a common group reporting structure. Accurate mapping remains an essential part of establishing consistent group reports.

Can a mixed Xero and QuickBooks Online group consolidate across currencies?

Yes. GATHER.nexus supports multi-currency consolidation and reporting, allowing entities with different functional currencies to be brought into a common reporting currency.

Is multi-ledger consolidation the same as currency translation?

No. Multi-ledger consolidation combines information from different accounting systems. Currency translation converts financial information into a common reporting currency. A group may require either or both.

Does connecting the ledgers complete the consolidation?

Connecting the ledgers supplies the underlying financial information. The finance team must also establish appropriate mappings, reporting settings, intercompany treatments and consolidation adjustments.
