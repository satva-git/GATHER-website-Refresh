Finally, the multi-entity layer comes together

Howard Thompson
November 3, 2026 1 min read
Back

  • Table of Content
  • Intercompany Workflow

GATHER.nexus now brings intercompany recharge, reconciliation and elimination into one connected workflow helping finance teams and outsourcers manage their Xero and QuickBooks groups with less repeated setup and stronger financial control.

A single integrated set up process across Intercompany

Create linked reconciliations and eliminations as you configure a recharge, carrying the relevant accounts, contacts and tracking selections through automatically.

Intercompany Control steps now integrate directly with Group Financial Reporting.

More powerful recharge and reconciliation tools

  • Bulk recharge setup: configure multiple ledger or vendor recharges in one journey.
  • Smarter recharge scheduling: use shared or individual recharge schedules, with separate timings for relevant recharge periods, reading final recharge amounts and posting transactions.
  • Supporting evidence: attach documents explaining allocation policies and markup decisions.
  • Stronger reconciliation control: flag reconciled transactions when underlying ledger data changes.

Monitor intercompany relationships across your group

A new Intercompany Overview shows which company relationships have recharges, reconciliations and eliminations in place – and highlights missing controls.

Open the linked records directly to review the setup or address a gap.

For in-house finance teams, this means faster configuration and easier oversight across entities.

For outsourcers, it supports more consistent delivery and simpler review across client groups.
