  • 2. How do I connect Xero to GATHER.nexus?

    Integrating the GATHER Platform for Xero is easy, by following these simple steps:

    1. Go to the Company section then click on ‘Add Xero company’.
    2. Click on “ Connect to Xero “.
    3. You will be prompted to log in to Xero using your Xero username and password.
    4. Choose ‘Company’
    5. After successfully connecting the company you will be redirected to the GATHER.nexus platform and fill the following details and click on “Add”.

    GATHER.nexus connects to your Xero organisation – you must authorise GATHER.nexus to access your data. You’ll only need to do this once for your organisation.
