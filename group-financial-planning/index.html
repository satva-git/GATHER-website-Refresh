2 Step 2: Roll Up Budgets to Create a Group Budget

Roll up Group Budgets

Roll up divisional, functional, or department level budgets natively into a single consolidated Group budget, replacing manual spreadsheet aggregation with a connected planning workflow aligned to your Group Reporting Template.

  • Native roll-up from divisional budgets to Group level
