The Fractional CFO role is a fast emerging one in the VC/PE backed space – and we only expect the move to Fractional arrangements to become ever more prevalent .

The GATHER Platform has been designed from the ground up as the ultimate solution to support you in your work .

  • Stop wasting time juggling manual processes and data sources – or multiple single purpose software solutions
  • Benefit from one powerful platform to solve all your multi-entity issues
  • Seamlessly handle multiple clients from a single platform with sophisticated access controls – then handover control to a full time CFO when the time comes
  • Stay in control of the status of your processes with our intuitive workflow management features
  • One click integration with the most popular SME accounting software packages – Xero and QuickBooks

The Problem

  • The move from a single entity environment to a multi-entity environment is typically challenging for Finance teams, creating additional workstreams for the consolidated Group and additional complexity in handling multiple currencies . 
  • Handling these additional requirements is time consuming and typically managed using manual or spreadsheet based solutions – or software applications which address only part of the work required.
  • The challenge is exacerbated for Fractional CFOs who look after the Finances of multiple clients and find themselves dealing with multiple different workflows across multiple different excel or software solutions . 

The GATHER Solution

  • The GATHER Platform is a comprehensive all in one solution to all your multi-entity requirements – covering:
    • Group Financial Reporting
    • Group Financial Planning
    • Intercompany Control – including Intercompany and Balance Sheet Control
  • The GATHER Platform smoothly handles multiple entities and multiple currencies across Financial Planning and Reporting; whilst our intercompany solution simplifies and automates the process of transfer pricing , management recharges and intercompany reconciliations .
  • The GATHER Platform allows Fractional CFOs to manage all their clients across a single platform with a simple and intuitive user interface .

The GATHER Edge

  • The GATHER Platform is designed by Finance and Operations executives experienced in scaling businesses
  • The platform provides a single comprehensive solution eliminating manual processes across three core finance activities
  • Each application on the GATHER Platform is designed according to the following principles:
    • Enable maximum configurability to give the user maximum control
    • Reduce (rather than create!) complexity
    • Start delivering value and saving time straight away

Multi-client Features for Fractional CFOs

  • Highly configurable access control functionality to ensure users (Client side or Fractional CFO side) have appropriate access levels (Review/Edit/Delete/Create) by:
    • Feature
    • Entity
    • Group
    • Group Class (Xero Tracking categories and/or QuickBooks classes)
  • Step by step on platform instructions for each individual feature to help new users upskill quickly
  • Industry and GAAP specific templates – which you can use and re-use for specific clients – or create your own
  • Total visibility and control over your client deliverables with our intuitive workflow functionality:
    • Clearly defined workflow steps such as:
      • Reviewing financial input data for validity and completeness with our Drill Down tools
      • Adding elimination or other adjusting journals
      • Reviewing digital working papers and signing off as complete
      • Producing Reports based on completed working papers
  • Tailor your working cadence according to individual client reporting requirements:
    • Monthly
    • Quarterly
    • Semi-annually
    • Annually
  • Allow selected stakeholders to access and “self-serve reports” – or securely share via email
