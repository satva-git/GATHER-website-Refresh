The role of the Finance team in a multi-entity business is a challenging one.

The movement from a single entity corporate structure to a multi-entity corporate structure – often driven by international expansion or by tax structuring/IP protection measures – presents its own set of challenges and additional responsibilities.

Fortunately, the GATHER Platform is here to smooth your journey – with all the help that you need to deal with Multi-Entity financial management on one single powerful platform.

  • Stop wasting time juggling manual processes and data sources – or multiple single purpose legacy software solutions
  • Benefit from one powerful platform to solve all your multi-entity issues
  • Keep your processes under control with our workflow management features
  • One click integration with the most popular SME accounting software packages – Xero and QuickBooks

The Problem

  • The move from a single entity environment to a multi-entity environment is typically challenging for Finance teams, creating additional workstreams for the consolidated Group and additional complexity in handling multiple currencies . 
  • Handling these additional requirements is time consuming and typically managed using manual or spreadsheet based solutions – or outdated software applications which address only part of the work required.
  • The challenge is exacerbated for Multi-entity Finance teams who find themselves dealing with multiple different workflows across multiple different excel or software solutions. 

The GATHER Solution

  • The GATHER Platform is a comprehensive all in one solution to all your multi-entity workflows – covering:
    • Group Financial Reporting
    • Group Financial Planning
    • Intercompany Control – including Intercompany Management and Control
  • The GATHER Platform smoothly handles multiple entities and multiple currencies across Financial Planning and Reporting; whilst our intercompany solution simplifies and automates the process of transfer pricing , management recharges and intercompany reconciliations .

The GATHER Edge

  • The GATHER Platform is designed by Finance and Operations executives experienced in scaling businesses
  • The platform provides a single comprehensive solution eliminating manual processes across three core finance activities
  • Each application on the GATHER Platform is designed according to the following principles:
    • Enable maximum configurability to give the user maximum control
    • Reduce (rather than create!) complexity
    • Start delivering value and saving time straight away

Features to Help Multi-entity Finance teams

  • One powerful Platform to solve all your multi-entity issues:
    • Multi-entity Consolidated Financial Reporting
    • Multi-Entity Consolidated Financial Planning
    • Intercompany Control
  • One click integration with the most popular SME accounting software packages – Xero and QuickBooks
  • Swifty onboard new team members – Step by step instructions for each individual feature on the GATHER Platform to help new team members to upskill quickly
  • Highly configurable access controls for Finance and non-Finance team members to enable the appropriate access (Review/Edit/Create/Delete) to the appropriate Features of the Platform (eg. Reports, Add Consolidation Journals)
  • Restrict access for non-Finance team members to Reports and functionality related to their own Division/Function only to enable self service Reports
  • Tailor your working cadence according to internal (such as management) or external (investors, banks, statutory) reporting requirements
    • Monthly
    • Quarterly
    • Semi-Annually
    • Annually
  • Maintain total visibility and control over your the Finance team’s deliverables with our intuitive workflow functionality: 
    • Clearly delineated workflow steps such as:
      • Reviewing financial input data for validity and completeness
      • Adding elimination or other adjusting journals
      • Reviewing digital working papers and signing off as Complete
      • Producing Reports based on Completed Working Papers
  • All your digital working papers accessible through a single intuitive dashboard
  • Securely share reports with selected stakeholders (eg. investors or members of the Management Team) via email or via access controls
