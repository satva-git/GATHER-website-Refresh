GATHER.nexus vs Spotlight Reporting: choosing software for Xero and QuickBooks multi-entity groups

Compare GATHER.nexus and Spotlight Reporting for Xero and QuickBooks groups, including consolidation, intercompany control, auditability and planning.

What is the difference between GATHER.nexus and Spotlight Reporting?

GATHER.nexus connects intercompany control, group financial reporting and group financial planning for businesses using Xero and QuickBooks Online. Spotlight Reporting provides management reporting, dashboards, consolidation and forecasting.

The practical choice depends on the work your finance team needs to manage. Spotlight Reporting is worth considering when the main requirement is presenting performance and preparing forecasts. GATHER.nexus is particularly relevant when finance teams also need to manage intercompany transactions, investigate reconciliation differences and substantiate consolidation adjustments within a connected workflow.

Both products offer consolidation. The distinction is how each supports the processes behind the group numbers.

Why intercompany control matters when comparing consolidation software

An elimination removes an internal balance or transaction from group reporting. A reconciliation establishes whether the entities agree about that balance or transaction. A recharge creates the underlying entries.

These are related tasks with different purposes. A group can produce an elimination while still having unresolved errors in its entity ledgers.

GATHER.nexus addresses the operational side through central posting, matching and correction workflows. Its intercompany tools support linked counterparty postings, configurable recharges and visibility of differences during the reporting period. Finance teams can validate reconciliation results against consolidation working papers.

For example, a parent company allocating shared costs to subsidiaries needs more than a consolidated expense total. The team needs to calculate the allocation, record the counterparties, investigate differences and verify the group treatment.

That makes intercompany activity an important selection criterion alongside the final report layout.

How do the platforms approach consolidation and auditability?

Spotlight Reporting explicitly supports consolidated reporting, multiple currencies and inter-entity eliminations. It should therefore be evaluated as a consolidation option, as well as a management reporting tool.

GATHER.nexus places consolidation working papers at the centre of the reporting process. Finance teams can review source data, inspect group-level journals and examine supporting calculations before completing the working papers and producing reports.

This is particularly relevant when a reviewer asks why a reported figure differs from the sum of the entity balances. A useful system needs to explain the adjustments as well as display the result.

GATHER.nexus also documents accounting for acquisitions and disposals, goodwill, fair value adjustments, partial ownership and non-controlling interests. Groups facing these scenarios should test their actual ownership structure and transactions during software evaluation.

The presence of an elimination feature alone does not demonstrate support for every complex consolidation scenario.

How do budgeting and forecasting compare?

Spotlight offers consolidated forecasting, including currency conversion and forecast eliminations. Its help centre explains that consolidated forecasts combine individual forecasts; a consolidation in Spotlight Reporting cannot currently be linked directly to Spotlight Forecasting.

GATHER.nexus supports budget preparation by division or function, native group roll-up and comparison with actual results.

These are different planning requirements. A group budgeting process and a three-way cash flow forecast should be tested separately. Finance teams should define whether they need delegated budget ownership, liquidity scenarios, or both.

Which platform should your group consider?

Consider Spotlight Reporting when your priorities centre on management reporting, branded reports, performance communication and cash flow forecasting.

Consider GATHER.nexus when your priorities include recurring intercompany activity, reconciliation control, consolidation working papers and complex group accounting alongside reporting and budgeting.

A useful demonstration should follow one transaction through its full journey: entity posting, counterparty reconciliation, group elimination, adjustment review and consolidated report.

Frequently asked questions

Is GATHER.nexus an alternative to Spotlight Reporting?

Yes, for Xero and QuickBooks Online groups evaluating consolidation and group reporting. The best fit depends on the importance of operational intercompany control, complex ownership accounting, report presentation and forecasting.

Does Spotlight Reporting support consolidation?

Yes. Spotlight documents consolidated reporting, multi-currency consolidation and inter-entity eliminations, as well as consolidated forecasting.

Why compare working papers as well as reports?

Reports present the outcome. Working papers explain how entity data, eliminations and other group adjustments produce that outcome. Reviewing both helps a finance team assess the close process.

How can I assess GATHER.nexus for my group?

Use a representative period containing intercompany activity, foreign-currency balances and any ownership adjustments. Explore GATHER.nexus Intercompany Control and Group Financial Reporting, then request a demonstration using your group’s requirements.
