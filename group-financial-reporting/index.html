Workflow Overview

A structured and audit-ready workflow for Consolidated Group Financial Reporting

From Group Reporting Template setup through AI assisted mapping, automated elimination journals, working papers review and validation, and final report generation, every step is connected in one auditable workflow.

GRT & AI Mapping

Define your Group Reporting Template and map entity GL accounts from Xero and QuickBooks with GATHER AI assistance.

Autojournals & Working Papers

Automated consolidation journals feed into a central Working Papers hub to create a permanent audit trail for eliminations, adjustments, and FX in Reserves tracking.

Transaction level drill down

Validate your financial data with multi-level drill down within your Working Papers – all the way from Group Financials to Transaction level data

Management, Investor and Statutory Reporting

Sign off your Working Papers once complete then create highly configurable Reports – with multi-level drill down to speed up variance analysis

Step 1: Create GRT

Build Your Group Reporting Template

Create Group Reporting Templates (GRT) according to your management team, investor and auditor requirements

  • Start from pre-defined industry templates or build a custom Group Reporting Template
  • Upload your own Group Reporting Template via CSV
  • Create multiple templates for a single Group for different audiences – management, investors, statutory reporting
  • Tailor existing templates to other client Groups/sub Groups

Map your GRT with the help of GATHER AI

AI-Assisted General Ledger Mapping

Map each entity’s General Ledger to your GRT with a drag-and-drop interface. GATHER AI recommends matches from underlying Xero or QuickBooks GL codes with a confidence score and clear logic, so mapping is fast, consistent, fully reviewable, and underpinned by a clear audit trail.

  • GATHER AI mapping recommendations with confidence scoring and clear audit trail
  • Drag-and-drop from entity General Ledger to Group Reporting Template codes
  • No financial data shared with GATHER AI
  • GATHER tracks new and unmapped ledger codes and alerts the user in our GRT summary screen

Automated Elimination Journals

Audit ready automated eliminations

Set up autojournals that generate elimination entries automatically each period, including intercompany eliminations, Foreign Exchange Differences in Reserves, and other recurring consolidation level adjustments.

  • Rules-based autojournal templates for recurring consolidation entries
  • Intercompany eliminations and Foreign Exchange Differences in Reserves journals generated automatically
  • Full audit trail of every autojournal calculation and posting, including Foreign Exchange Differences in Reserves

Step 4: Review Working Papers

Multi-level drill down for data validation and audit traceability

Working Papers is the central hub for Finance teams to review and sign off on the integrity of their Group level close

  • Validate the underlying Entity level data with Drill down by Entity, by General Ledger Code and by individual transaction
  • Click through to Xero/QuickBooks Online to review or amend individual transactions
  • Full audit trail of consolidation level journals
  • Full backing calculations for all FX Differences in Reserves postings
  • Sign off Consolidation Working Papers as completed – and ready to generate Reports

Step 5: Manual Topside Adjustments

GAAP adjustments, reclassifications and other Group level topside adjustments

Fully integrated manual journal functionality allows Finance teams to make any further required adjustments to their Group level close – from GAAP adjustments to reclassifications

  • Full audit record of manual topside adjustments – meaning GATHER is the trusted System of Record – rather than spreadsheets
  • Integrated P&L and Balance Sheet Journals with carry forward impact and roll forward to Earnings in Reserves
  • Set up one-off or recurring journals – and tag journals by Type
  • Completely removes the need for spreadsheets – even in the most complex scenarios

Step 6: Generate Reports

Highly configurable Reports – with multi-level Drill down for ease of variance analysis

Produce consolidated group reports directly from validated working papers, with industry-specific templates and formats your board, investors, and auditors expect. Every report line connects back to source data through the same connected workflow.

  • Industry-specific consolidated Group Reporting Templates
  • Reports generated from validated Working Papers, with Completion Status flags
  • Full multi-level drill down from consolidated financials to transaction level data to accelerate variance analysis
  • Full flexibility around Reporting Presentation currency
