About us

GATHER.nexus was founded by Howard Thompson, a scale-up CFO , based on his own experiences of building finance functions across seven countries and four continents for a rapidly growing VC backed business . The GATHER Platform is designed from the ground up for Fractional CFOs and Finance Teams in scaling businesses – and is aimed, in particular, at solving frictions for Finance teams working with multi-entity (and therefore typically multi-currency) businesses .

The GATHER Platform integrates with Xero and QuickBooks – two of the most popular accounting packages for growing businesses .

The Platform offers three modules – each solving a particular multi-entity finance issue:

  • Group Financial Reporting
  • Group Financial Control – including Intercompany Control
  • Group Financial Planning

Meanwhile, Fractional CFOs will enjoy the opportunity to manage all their clients on a single platform with our sophisticated access control and account handover features to enable maximum flexibility .

We hope you enjoy exploring the GATHER Platform!
