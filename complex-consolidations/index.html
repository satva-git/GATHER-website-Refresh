The Challenge

Accounting for complex group structures and transactions typically necessitates spreadsheet workarounds meaning the spreadsheet becomes your System of Record

The Solution

GATHER.nexus automates the accounting for complex consolidation scenarios with a complete audit trail, meaning Finance teams are no longer reliant on laborious manual calculations and spreadsheets for critical Financial Reporting processes.
